# Datasheet

Short datasheet for the data Escudo runs on (after *Datasheets for Datasets*, Gebru et al.).

## Motivation
A talk demo needs a car-insurance claim that agents can run end to end in under three minutes, on
stage, with nothing real at risk. All data is fictional and exists only to make that scenario run.

## Composition
- **1 customer** (Andrea Morales, `cus_demo`) with **1 active policy** (`POL-GT-2027-0042`): Toyota
  Corolla 2022, plate `P-512KLM`; own damage, third party, towing, 7-day replacement car; USD 500
  deductible. Theft is deliberately *not* covered, so `check_coverage` has a "no" to show.
- **3 repair shops** in Guatemala City (name, address, rating 4.5–4.8, labor factor, slot hours) and
  **2 tow providers** (base location, speed, dispatch time). Names are invented; any match with a real
  business is accidental. Coordinates are approximate city locations.
- **3 crash photos** of the insured car (minor rear damage), generated; one was regenerated so no real
  store brand appears (only the car's own badge).
- **5 spoken Spanish clips** (TTS) for voice tests.
- **Runtime records** created by the scenario: claims, approvals, timeline events (schema
  `contracts/events.schema.json`). `make reset` deletes them; device registrations survive a reset.

## Collection
Nothing collected. Seed values are hand-written in `backend/escudo/seed.py`; media is generated by the
scripts listed in `data/SOURCES.md`, with prompts and seeds committed.

## Personal data
None. The customer, email (`andrea.morales@example.com`, reserved domain), plate and phone numbers are
fictional. Timeline events carry only display-safe summaries.

## Uses and limits
For demos, tests and rehearsals of agent ↔ app integration. Quotes, ETAs and the damage assessment
are plausible, not actuarial: the assessor is a language model reading generated photos, and repair
quotes are computed from its cost range. Not suitable for training or evaluating real claims models.

## Maintenance
Regenerate with `make seed`, `make photos`, `make voices`, `make brand`. Slots are relative to the
day you seed, so the data stays current without edits.
