Presenter timeline view of "Escudo", a car insurer whose AI agents handle a minor-crash claim, designed for a 1920x1080 projector in an auditorium, large type readable from the back row. Background very pale green-gray #F3F6F4, ink #14201C, primary deep pine green #0F5C4D, warm sand #E9DFC8 as a secondary surface, amber #9A5B00 on pale amber #FFF1D6 for approval pauses. One geometric-humanist sans family (like Onest) in several weights.
